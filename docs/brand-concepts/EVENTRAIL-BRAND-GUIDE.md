# EventRail identity

## The mark

The junction symbol represents an event as the shared point where attendees, organizers, vendors, and venues connect. Its four arms keep the idea legible as a compact site and app icon; the marigold center marks the event itself.

## Approved artwork

- `public/brand/eventrail-symbol.svg` — full-colour master symbol.
- `public/brand/web/eventrail-black.svg` and `eventrail-white.svg` — one-colour versions.
- `public/brand/web/eventrail-mono-176b5b.svg` — single-colour rail green.
- `public/brand/web/eventrail-square.svg` — square transparent symbol.
- `public/brand/web/eventrail-app-icon.svg` — symbol on the green app tile.
- Root `public/favicon*`, `public/apple-touch-icon.png`, and `public/icon-*.png` — installed web icon set.

The website lockup pairs the symbol with the EventRail name in the existing Bricolage Grotesque interface wordmark. Figtree remains the body and interface typeface. Both fonts are already self-hosted by the project.

## Palette

| Colour | HEX | RGB | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rail green | `#176B5B` | 23, 107, 91 | Main arms and brand anchor |
| Marigold | `#E8B34B` | 232, 179, 75 | Event centre; small accent only |
| Ink | `#172A26` | 23, 42, 38 | Dark backgrounds and text |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | 255, 255, 255 | Reversed mark and open space |

## Use

- Leave clear space around the symbol equal to the diameter of its center circle.
- Use the full-colour symbol on white or a pale neutral. Use the white version on rail green or ink.
- Use the simplified square or favicon artwork at 24 px and below.
- Keep the symbol at least 16 px high on screen; use a larger size for print.
- Keep the name in the supplied website wordmark styling. Do not stretch, rotate, add effects, or introduce extra colours.

The symbol was rendered and checked at 16, 32, and 64 px. Trademark availability has not been assessed.
